## Brand & Style

This design system embodies bespoke botanical luxury, architectural restraint, and clinical botanical rigor. The aesthetic balances apothecary heritage with contemporary high-fashion editorial clarity. The target demographic seeks uncompromising efficacy paired with sensory sophistication; the interface should evoke deliberate slowness, tactile confidence, and quiet authority.

The visual style is **Minimalist Editorial Luxury**:
- Expansive, breathing negative space that treats the screen as archival print paper.
- Razor-thin structural divisions referencing apothecary labels and luxury packaging inserts.
- Restrained, hyper-precise micro-interactions prioritizing ease and fluid deceleration over bouncing effects.
- Utilitarian metadata paired with poetic, expressive typographic moments.

## Colors

The palette references raw botanicals, warm stone, and refined metal leaf. The dominant visual canvas is strictly warm, rejecting sterile optical whites in favor of linen and parchment tones.

- **Primary (`#1A1817` - Deep Espresso Charcoal):** Grounded anchor for high-contrast headlines, key actions, framing outlines, and authoritative editorial copy.
- **Secondary (`#767F6D` - Muted Botanical Olive):** Evokes dried herbarium leaves, cold-pressed tinctures, and conscious earthiness. Applied to secondary tags, active filter states, and ambient badges.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A880` - Warm Champagne Gold):** Reserved for delicate metallic accents, key active states, batch badges, and refined micro-elements.
- **Neutral Surface Hierarchy:**
  - Base Canvas: Warm Alabaster (`#FBF9F5`)
  - Elevated Container / Secondary Panel: Rich Sand Cream (`#F4EFEB`)
  - Structural Hairlines & Borders: Subdued Linen (`#E7E1D8`)
  - Muted Editorial Body: Soft Ash Charcoal (`#4A4643`)
  - Subdued Metadata: Warm Slate (`#827E79`)

Do not introduce artificial gradients, multi-stop drop shadows, or saturated alert tones. Error states use a subdued ochre-brick (`#8E3B33`); success states resolve naturally into the olive secondary token.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a conversation between literary classicism and Swiss modernist functionalism:

- **Playfair Display** introduces romantic, high-contrast, editorial drama. Headlines should favor regular weight (`400`) and italic variants for pull quotes, biological extract names, or emotional cadence. Avoid bold weights in large display roles to maintain elegance.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a clean, neutral, and highly legible counterweight for descriptive literature, formulation details, dosages, and user actions.
- **Micro Typography & Tracking:** All functional labels, categorizations, badges, and button labels must be set in uppercase with extensive tracking (`0.18em` to `0.25em`). This mimics architectural typesetting and serialized batch labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors a hardbound art monograph: structured, disciplined, and unapologetically airy.

- **Grid Framework:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with generous margins (`4.5rem`) and structural gutters (`2.5rem`). Content sections span asymmetrical divisions (e.g., 5-column editorial text block paired with a 7-column architectural product view).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `2.5rem` margins.
  - **Mobile (under 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Section Rhythm:**
  - Substantial vertical separation (`space-2xl` / 7rem) between thematic collections and editorial articles allows single formulations to breathe individually.
  - Asymmetric pacing: alternating full-bleed imagery with contained, disciplined text modules aligned to hairline columns.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects simulated drop shadows, 3D skeuomorphism, and blurred layers. Instead, depth is achieved exclusively through **tonal transitions and hair-thin structural divisions**:

- **Tonal Planes:** Foreground cards, drawers, and modal surfaces transition from Alabaster (`#FBF9F5`) to Cream Sand (`#F4EFEB`).
- **Razor Hairlines:** Elements are bounded by crisp, 1px continuous borders (`#E7E1D8`). These outlines create the feel of apothecary display shelving and clinical ingredient cards.
- **Shadow Policy:** Traditional shadows are entirely prohibited across standard UI surfaces. For detached modal overlays or floating navigation bars, use an imperceptible ambient presence: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(26, 24, 23, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The shape system enforces pure architectural geometry with a roundedness setting of **0 (Sharp)**:

- Every card, button, input field, modal, and drawer terminates in precise, clean 90-degree right angles (`border-radius: 0px`).
- Sharp geometries echo Le Labo label cuts, luxury presentation boxes, and minimalist architectural flagships.
- Exceptions are strictly limited to intrinsic circular elements: quantity counters, spherical color swatches, or radio dots.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Espresso Charcoal (`#1A1817`) fill with Warm Alabaster (`#FBF9F5`) typography. Strictly uppercase, tracked `label-caps-md`. Padding: `16px 32px`. Hover state: smooth shift to Botanical Olive (`#767F6D`) over 300ms.
- **Secondary / Outline Action:** Transparent background with 1px hairline border in `#1A1817`. Deep Espresso text. Hover state: fills completely with `#1A1817`, shifting text to `#FBF9F5`.
- **Text Link / Editorial CTA:** Uppercase tracked label with a razor-thin 1px underline positioned 6px below text. Underline expands outward from center on hover.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- Flat presentation enclosed within a 1px border (`#E7E1D8`) or floating seamlessly on the `#FBF9F5` canvas.
- Image containers feature a quiet, sand-toned background (`#F4EFEB`) with aspect ratios of 4:5 or 3:4.
- Content stack: Formula volume/batch number in `label-caps-sm`, botanical headline in `headline-sm`, summary in `body-sm`, and price right-aligned in `body-md`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Single baseline border (1px `#E7E1D8`) rather than four-sided enclosures for a cleaner, notebook-inspired look.
- Floating placeholder set in `label-caps-md` that transitions cleanly above the line upon focus.
- Focus state: hairline color transitions from `#E7E1D8` to `#1A1817` with no glow or rings.
- Checkboxes: Exact 16x16px squares with 1px border. Checked state renders a solid inner square in `#1A1817` with 3px internal margin.

### Tags, Badges & Chips
- Sharp rectangles with 1px borders.
- Padding: `6px 12px`.
- Typography: `label-caps-sm`.
- Muted Olive version: Tinted background (`rgba(118, 127, 109, 0.1)`) with `#767F6D` border and text for active ingredient callouts.

### Botanical Formula Accords & Ingredient Accordions
- Horizontal hairline dividers (`1px solid #E7E1D8`) between accordion items.
- Item labels set in `headline-sm` with a slender `+` / `−` mechanical indicator.
- Expansion reveals two columns: scientific extract terminology alongside consumer sensorial descriptions.
